Only VAT vendors issue tax invoices. If you are not registered for VAT, do not charge VAT or show a VAT number. Send an ordinary invoice instead.

Common mistakes that get input VAT claims disallowed

  • A missing or wrong VAT number for the supplier, or for a VAT-registered client on invoices over R5 000.
  • Vague descriptions such as "services rendered" with no detail of what was supplied.
  • Re-using or skipping invoice numbers, or issuing a second "original" for the same supply instead of a copy, credit note or debit note.
  • Showing only a total with no VAT amount and no statement of the VAT rate.
  • Invoicing a local, standard-rated supply in a foreign currency.
