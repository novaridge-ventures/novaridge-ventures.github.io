How it works

  1. Email novaridgeventures@gmail.com with the service you want and any files or notes.
  2. We reply with a short written scope, the price, and the turnaround. Nothing starts until you confirm.
  3. You pay the deposit (for a website) or we begin the smaller job and invoice on delivery.
  4. We deliver the files and, for a website, a live preview link. You review, we make one round of reasonable corrections, then you pay the balance if one is due.

Payment

Invoices are in South African rand. You can pay by EFT into the account on the invoice, or with a PayPal invoice sent to your email. We do not take payment in this chat or through an unofficial link. Work stays yours after the invoice is paid.

A fair limit on the promise

These offers cover the scope above. If your job is larger (a shop with checkout, a custom app, or someone needing to be on your network), we will say so and give you a separate quote, or decline. We do not pretend that a brochure website or a spreadsheet is a full operations system.
